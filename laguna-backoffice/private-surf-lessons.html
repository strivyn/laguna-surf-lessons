How the lesson runs

Troy texts you the spot, picked on the conditions, safety and your ability. You suit up and start on the sand with the pop-up, then you are in the water with Troy beside you.

He picks the wave, times the push and tells you the one thing to think about.

Who it is for

First-timers who want to stand up today, nervous swimmers who want one instructor watching only them, and anyone who wants to learn faster than a group allows.

Ages five and up. You should be comfortable in water over your head.
